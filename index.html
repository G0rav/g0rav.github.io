02Aftershoot
>99% accuracy

Face detection on 8000×8000 px photos

Finding tiny faces in huge professional photos, on-device.

TensorFlowONNXSageMaker
Details

Problem

High-resolution photos hold huge amounts of detail and very small faces, which off-the-shelf detectors miss.

Approach

To build the dataset, I split each image into 16 overlapping tiles so every face was covered, and annotated them with an open-source detector. I then designed a custom architecture for tiny faces at high resolution. At inference, a 4-tile split kept detection fast and accurate.

Impact

Face-detection complaints dropped sharply, and users began recommending the product for its reliability.

03Aftershoot
24% → 80%

Kiss detection

Detecting a tiny moment inside a huge image, with heavy class imbalance.

CNNTensorFlowFeature Engineering
Details

Problem

Classify photos as "kiss", "almost kiss" or "no kiss". The moment fills a tiny region of a ~6K image, and the first model scored 24%, worse than random (33%).

Approach

I used face detections to find the region of interest, smart-cropped around it, and trained a CNN on the crops. Weighted loss and down-sampling handled the imbalance (15K kiss images against ~100K for each other class).

Impact

Accuracy rose to about 80%, and the preprocessing made the whole application about 10% faster.

05Aftershoot
2,400–9,000K

White balance prediction for Lightroom

Predicting the right temperature and tint for every photo.

CNNRegressionTransfer Learning
Details

Lightroom's temperature range runs from 2,000K to 50,000K, but my analysis showed that changes outside roughly 2,400–9,000K barely affect the image. I focused a regression model on that range: a transfer-learned CNN that takes the image plus its current temperature and tint. It made white-balance correction faster for photographers.
